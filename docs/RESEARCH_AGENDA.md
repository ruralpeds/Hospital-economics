### **PROJECT 1: NICU Economics & Quality-Value Analysis**

**Status:** Ready to expand  
**Primary Research Questions:**
- What is the cost-effectiveness of evidence-based NICU interventions?
- How do physiological outcomes map to economic value?
- What is the cost per QALY gained for quality improvements?

**Research Components:**

1. **Surfactant Therapy Economics**
   - Cost per life-year saved
   - Impact on respiratory morbidity
   - Cost-effectiveness vs. placebo/standard care
   - Long-term follow-up (cost of BPD, neurodevelopmental impairment)

2. **Ventilation Strategy Comparison** (Already have NICU example)
   - Conventional vs. HFOV (cost-effectiveness)
   - Gentle ventilation protocols
   - Non-invasive ventilation (CPAP)
   - Cost per outcome prevented (IVH, BPD)

3. **Early Intervention & Outcomes**
   - Prophylactic antibiotics economics
   - Early feeding protocols
   - Kangaroo care implementation costs & benefits
   - Breast milk vs. formula economics

4. **Physiological-Economic Integration** (PedNeoSim coupling)
   - Link respiratory function → costs
   - Predict long-term outcomes from early physiology
   - Optimize ventilation strategy for cost-effectiveness
   - Risk stratification for intervention targeting

**Expected Outputs:**
- 3-5 peer-reviewed publications
- Multi-hospital economic data repository
- Clinical decision support tools
- Policy briefs for NICU directors

**Primary Research Questions:**
- What is the ROI of care coordination programs?
- Which patient populations benefit most?
- What is the cost per readmission prevented?
- How do results vary across settings/payers?

**Research Components:**

1. **Program Effectiveness Analysis**
   - Readmission rates (30-day, 90-day)
   - ED utilization changes
   - Hospital readmission costs avoided
   - Quality of life improvements

2. **Cost Analysis**
   - Program implementation costs
   - Ongoing operational costs
   - Cost per patient reached
   - Cost per readmission prevented
   - ROI by patient subgroup

3. **Comparative Programs**
   - Standard care coordination
   - Enhanced coordination + telehealth
   - Risk-stratified intensive intervention
   - Multi-payer programs

4. **Variation Analysis**
   - By diagnosis (CHF, COPD, pneumonia, CKD)
   - By demographics (age, race, insurance)
   - By setting (urban, suburban, rural)
   - By payer (Medicare, Medicaid, Commercial)

5. **Implementation Insights**
   - Barriers to adoption
   - Success factors
   - Scalability assessment
   - Sustainability analysis

- Cost calculator tool

### **PROJECT 5: Value-Based Care Contracting & Financial Sustainability**

**Status:** Ready to launch  
**Primary Research Questions:**
- How financially sustainable are different VBC contract structures?
- What upfront investments are required?
- What quality improvements are necessary?
- How do results compare across patient populations and settings?

**Research Components:**

1. **Payment Model Economics**
   - Fee-for-service baseline
   - Shared savings arrangements (80/20, 70/30)
   - Capitation models
   - Bundled payment scenarios
   - Risk-sharing arrangements

2. **Financial Impact Modeling**
   - Upfront implementation costs
   - Cost of care reduction needed
   - Quality metric achievement
   - Break-even timeline
   - 3-year financial projections

3. **Quality Improvement Requirements**
   - Readmission reduction targets
   - ED utilization reduction
   - Preventive care metrics
   - Patient satisfaction
   - Cost per quality improvement

4. **Payer Perspective Analysis**
   - Cost savings from VBC
   - Transition costs
   - Long-term financial impact
   - Portfolio impact (mix of FFS + VBC)

5. **Provider Perspective Analysis**
   - Implementation investment
   - Infrastructure needed
   - Care redesign requirements
   - Downside risk and mitigation
   - Sustainability of model

6. **Multi-Scenario Analysis**
   - Conservative (minimal savings)
   - Base case (expected savings)
   - Optimistic (strong execution)
   - Sensitivity on key parameters

**Expected Outputs:**
- Peer-reviewed publication: "Financial Sustainability of Value-Based Care Transitions"
- Decision support tools for contract negotiation
- Health system business case templates
- Policy recommendations for payers/regulators

### **PROJECT 6: Real-World Data Cost-Effectiveness Studies**

**Status:** Framework ready, data collection pending  
**Primary Research Questions:**
- What are real-world costs and outcomes in typical practice?
- How do they differ from clinical trial results?
- What patient characteristics predict high cost?
- How can providers identify high-value interventions?

**Research Components:**

1. **EHR-Based Cost Analysis**
   - Extract cost data from hospital billing systems
   - Link to clinical data from EHR
   - Validate and clean data
   - Risk-adjust for case mix

2. **Real-World Effectiveness**
   - Actual patient outcomes (vs. trial-selected populations)
   - Long-term follow-up costs
   - Readmission and complication rates
   - Quality of life (patient-reported)

3. **Comparative Effectiveness**
   - Alternative treatments in practice
   - Practice variation analysis
   - Provider performance variation
   - Cost drivers identification

4. **Subgroup Analysis**
   - By age, sex, race, insurance
   - By comorbidities and severity
   - By geographic region
   - By provider type (hospital, health system)

5. **Health Equity Analysis**
   - Cost-effectiveness by demographic group
   - Access and utilization disparities
   - Health outcome variation
   - Intervention efficacy heterogeneity

**Expected Outputs:**
- 3-5 peer-reviewed publications
- Health system cost-effectiveness dashboards
- Open-source real-world data analysis repository
- Equity assessment frameworks
